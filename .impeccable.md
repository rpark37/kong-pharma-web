## Design Context

### Users
Investors, pharma business-development teams and CRO partners evaluating Kong's Pharmaceutical, usually on a laptop and often from a forwarded link. Secondary: clinicians and scientists checking the science and the pipeline.

### Brand Personality
Warm, exacting, hopeful. Founder-led and human about patients, with measured claims and figure-quality detail; no hype.

### Aesthetic Direction
Refine the existing lab aesthetic: deep-teal slate for the dark narrative sections (Science, Partnership, Contact), warm off-white paper for the light ones. Teal is the single brand accent; copper is the one secondary (partner marks, status). Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque (display), Albert Sans (body), Red Hat Mono (labels). Anti-references: neon dashboards, glassmorphism, purple-blue gradients, generic SaaS card grids.

### Design Principles
1. Teal is rare and meaningful: one accent, used for action and key figures only.
2. Surfaces are solid and tinted, never blurred or glowing.
3. One type system: display / body / mono with a 5-step scale and real hierarchy.
4. Rhythm over uniformity: vary spacing and layout; not everything is a card.
5. Motion is orchestrated, not ambient: a few staggered entrances, expo-out easing.
