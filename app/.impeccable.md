# Impeccable

## Design Context

### Users

Kong's Labs is a **portfolio and technical demonstration**. Kong's Pharmaceutical is the
fictional frame that gives the work a coherent subject; the work itself is the point.

Readers are hiring managers, collaborators and other engineers. They arrive to see whether the
person who built this can do graphics and data visualization at a high level, and they read code
as fluently as they read the page. The job they are doing is *evaluation* — quickly, and often
from a link someone else sent them.

The target reaction is **"how was this made?"**, never "which AI made this?". Provenance is part
of the product: the README names every source it recreates (the MorphCharts client, OHDSI Ares,
the GA4 Merchandise sample, human-atlas, God's Eye View), and pages credit their origins in the
UI. Keep doing that — honest attribution reads as confidence, not as a disclaimer.

### Brand Personality

**Dense · engineered · atmospheric.**

It should read like a mission console or a sensor feed: high information density, motion used as
signal rather than decoration, a dark instrument ground. Nothing here is trying to be friendly or
reassuring. It is trying to look like equipment that works.

Restraint is what separates this from pastiche. The FUI vocabulary is convincing because the
numbers are real and the layout obeys rules — the moment a readout is decorative, the whole thing
reads as a screensaver.

### Aesthetic Direction

Established and working:

- **The FUI vocabulary** in `app/src/app/shared/fui/fui-panels.ts` — 19 canvas primitives:
  uppercase mono, dot-path identifiers, corner brackets never closed into rectangles, key/value
  pairs, empty slots drawn as dashes so absent data still occupies its cell.
- **Motion is centralised** in `shared/animation/motion.ts` — Quad eases only, shared durations
  and staggers. Components call `GsapService` rather than choosing eases ad hoc.
- **Every visualization is a specification.** Vega / Vega-Lite for 2D, MorphCharts' Vega-derived
  grammar for 3D. The atlas's Anatomy mode is the one deliberate exception.
- **Typography**: Rajdhani for display, IBM Plex Sans / IBM Plex Mono for body and data,
  self-hosted in `app/public/fonts/`.

Anti-references: consumer-SaaS friendliness, glassmorphism as decoration, anything that looks
like a template. A visitor should not be able to name the framework from the styling.

### Theme: two surfaces, decided 2026-09-18

**The light/dark split is deliberate. Do not unify it.**

Brand voice and page surface are separate questions, and conflating them is what made this look
like drift. The voice is dark, cinematic and instrument-like throughout. The *surface* follows
what each page is for:

| Surface | Pages | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Light** — paper, ink, teal accent | the chart pages: `ares`, `merchandise`, `google`, `bayes`, `vega-charts`, `sanddance-specs`, `morphcharts`, `transition`, `science`, `atlas` | Dense analytical reports with a lot of small text and sustained reading. Light is the right ground for that, which is why the migration happened. |
| **Dark** — ink instrument palette | the three.js FUI family: `hud`, `site-map`, `controls`, `gev` | Consoles, not documents. Read at a glance, atmosphere is part of the content. |

Three reasons this is the right call and not a compromise:

1. **The split is an asset for a portfolio.** It demonstrates range — a clean editorial data-viz
   app *and* a dark instrument console. A single voice would show less, not more polish.
2. **Reversing it would be the third pass over the same surface** (dark → light → dark) inside a
   day. The light theme landed deliberately via
   `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-09-18-light-theme.md`.
3. **The cost lands in the worst place.** `fui-panels.ts` hardcodes teal rgba literals inside 19
   primitives used by four pages — the same knot that forced the God's Eye View sensor modes to
   become an SVG filter rather than a palette swap. Plus `PALETTE_LIGHT`, 33 custom properties in
   `styles/_tokens.scss`, and four canvas pages.

**Amended 2026-09-22: a user-chosen dark theme now exists for the light pages.** The nav's
theme key (`ThemeService`, `[data-theme="dark"]` tokens in `styles/_tokens.scss`, `PALETTE_DARK`)
switches every chart page to a dark instrument surface with a view-transition crossfade, and the
scenes that bake colours into a spec (`shared/theme/surface.ts`) rebuild. Light stays the default
and the FUI consoles stay dark regardless — the split above still describes the *defaults*; the
toggle is the reader's override, not a unification.

`app.ts` already carries this as a comment on the nav grouping; this is the fuller statement of
it. The colour work committed in `d2d0088` tunes the **light** palette specifically — a hue that
passes on paper is not validated for a dark surface, so anything moving between the two must be
re-stepped (`validate_palette.js --mode dark`).

### Open questions

**Typography is due a second look.** IBM Plex Sans and IBM Plex Mono are impeccable's named
training-data defaults. They are doing real work here and are not urgent to replace, but a
portfolio whose stated goal is distinctiveness should not use the two most predictable technical
typefaces. Rajdhani is the genuinely characterful choice and could carry more. Filed as someday:
swapping typefaces across fifteen pages is a large diff for a small gain.

**`sci.title` has no translation.** `index.html:125` carries `data-i18n="sci.title"` with no entry
in `i18n.js` — 172 keys used, exactly one missing — so the `<h2>Macropinocytosis</h2>` heading
stays English in all seven languages while its six siblings translate. `setLang()` returns early
on a null lookup, so it fails silently. Deferred deliberately when CLAUDE.md was written; the
translations want a human eye, since CLAUDE.md flags the medical copy as machine-generated.

### Design Principles

1. **The data is the reason.** Every readout on screen is backed by something real — a captured
   snapshot, a propagated orbit, a measured value. Decorative telemetry is the failure mode this
   aesthetic dies of.
2. **Accessibility: reasonable effort, deliberately documented.** Charts stay accessible —
   legends, contrast, table views, a palette that passes CVD validation. The FUI canvases are
   accepted as decorative, but each one carries a label and a short description rather than
   presenting as an unlabelled canvas. Where a gap is accepted, it is written down, not ignored.

   **Accepted gaps, decided 2026-09-18 — do not re-litigate without new information:**
   - The four canvas pages (`hud`, `site-map`, `controls`, `gev`) paint every readout to a
     bitmap, so none of it reaches the accessibility tree. Each carries `role="img"` and a
     one-sentence `aria-label`; the content behind it has no text equivalent and is not keyboard
     reachable. Accepted because the readouts are atmosphere, not information a visitor needs.
     - **Resolved for `gev` the same day.** It was flagged here as the weakest of the four, on the
       grounds that its contacts rail carries real data and is the page's primary control. That
       rail is now a `cdkListbox` in real DOM over the canvas, with roving tabindex, arrow/Home/End
       keys and a `LiveAnnouncer`; the scene no longer paints it. `@angular/cdk` is the only
       dependency this added, and its runtime stays in the lazy page chunk.
     - The test for the remaining three is the same one `gev` failed: does the canvas carry data a
       visitor needs, or only atmosphere? `hud`, `site-map` and `controls` are still atmosphere.
       If one gains a real control, it earns the same treatment rather than a louder `aria-label`.
   - Interactive controls sit near 26px rather than the 44×44 target. Accepted: desktop portfolio.
   - No skip link. `<nav aria-label="Pages">` and `<main>` landmarks are present and cover most of
     the need.
   - Not verified: screen-reader behaviour. The `aria-label`s above have been written but never
     heard. Claim them as working only after someone listens with VoiceOver.
3. **Shared vocabulary over private copies.** Layout, motion and drawing primitives live in
   `styles.scss`, `motion.ts` and `fui-panels.ts`. Three private copies of a pattern drift within
   a week — this has already happened once and was corrected.
4. **Offline by default.** Assets are vendored so pages work with no network. A page that needs a
   third party at runtime is a page that will be broken when someone opens the link.
5. **Show the seams on purpose.** Name the sources, keep the capture scripts in the repo, let the
   commit messages explain the trade. The engineering being legible is part of what is being
   demonstrated.
